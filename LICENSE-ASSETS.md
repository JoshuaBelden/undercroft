## What counts as assets

- Everything under `static/assets/`: models, textures, animations, sounds, music and images.
- Raw art sources (`art/`, not in this repository): Blender files, exports, recordings, image sources.
- The game's writing: lore, the Affliction, item and rite text, hints and notes, wherever they appear (including as strings in `src/lib/content/`).
- The name **Undercroft** as the name of this game, and its logos.

## What you may do with them

You may download, use and modify the assets **only to develop, test and contribute to Undercroft itself**: run the game on your own machine, make changes, and send them back as contributions.

## What you may not do

Without written permission from the copyright holder, you may not:

- host or run the game, or a modified version of it, for other people with these assets;
- redistribute the assets outside this repository or its forks made for contributing;
- use them, or the Undercroft name, in any other project, game or product, commercial or not.

A fork of the code is welcome under the AGPL. It needs its own art, sound, writing and name.
