# Undercroft: Lore

Working document. **Established** facts come from design decisions. **Proposed** items are open ideas, not canon until confirmed.

## The Catacombs

**Established**

- A vast network beneath the city: ossuaries, old quarries, flooded cisterns, chapels, crawlspaces, and sealed passages.
- Explorers enter through known entrances. Each expedition begins in a start room with stairs or a rope leading down.
- The deeper you go, the older and more dangerous it gets, and the more valuable what's left behind.

**Proposed**

- The catacombs are older than the city above. The bones were placed there as a seal, not just a burial.
- Layers by age: modern maintenance tunnels → the ossuary → medieval crypts → Roman quarry → something older.

## Afflictions

**Established**

- Afflictions are forces that take hold of the catacombs. They are not single creatures; they change the place itself.
- Each Affliction brings its own Thralls, conditions, and a rite that can resolve it.
- Left alone too long, an Affliction **manifests** and hunts.
- A manifestation can be banished temporarily, but only the rite truly resolves an Affliction.

**Proposed**

- Afflictions are what the catacombs suffer when something below stirs, like an infection rising through the layers.
- Each Affliction began with someone: a person whose death, sin, or bargain gave it a foothold.

## Gloaming

**Established**

- The first Affliction explorers face.
- It darkens the catacombs. Its domain is half-light, between light and dark.
- Resolved by a rite requiring five candles, chalk, and the Pendant.
- Its Thralls look like ordinary people with black eyes, seen only in dim light, standing in corners.

**Proposed**

- The Pendant belonged to the person the Gloaming began with.
- That person was lost in the dark below and waited, light failing, until something answered.
- The Thralls are those the Gloaming has taken: explorers and people who wandered down and never came back.

## Thralls

**Established**

- Creatures bound to an Affliction. Infrequent, but terrifying.

**Proposed**

- Thralls were once people. What remains of them is the Affliction wearing them.

## Rites

**Established**

- Rites resolve Afflictions. Their requirements are learned from clues left in the catacombs.
- Knowledge of a rite carries across expeditions.

**Proposed**

- The clues were left by earlier explorers and an older order who once kept the catacombs sealed.

## Shrines

**Established**

- Found in the catacombs. Each protects one item for an explorer, through death or escape.

**Proposed**

- Shrines are remnants of the order that once kept the seal. What's placed in them is under their protection.

## The explorers (players)

**Established**

- Players run repeated expeditions, sell what they recover, and buy and repair gear.

**Proposed**

- Who sends them, and who buys what they bring up: collectors, the Church, or a fence who asks no questions.
- Why they keep going down.

## The hub

**Proposed**

- A place above ground near a known entrance, run by whoever buys relics and sells gear.

## Open questions

- What lies at the bottom of the catacombs?
- Who left the clues and built the shrines?
- Is there a single source behind all Afflictions?
- What is the city above, and does it matter?
- What do the explorers call themselves?
