# Release notes

What changed in each version of Undercroft, newest first. Players see these in game on the About page. Write them for players: what you'll notice, not how it was built. `pnpm release` turns the Unreleased section into the next version (see [docs/dev/releasing.md](docs/dev/releasing.md)).

## Unreleased

The first closed beta. Thank you for testing.

### Descend

- Solo, or co-op with up to four explorers. Make an account, find friends by gamer tag and invite them to your party.
- Every expedition is a new catacomb, built from a seed: tunnels, burial rooms, ossuaries, a flooded cistern, stairs down to deeper levels, crawlspaces and squeezes.
- Movement: walk, sprint, crouch, crawl, swim, dive, mantle onto ledges. Over-the-shoulder view, or first person.
- Your headlamp is the only light. Its battery runs down, and the dark wears on your sanity.

### The Gloaming

- The first Affliction. The longer you stay, the worse it gets: watch for the omens.
- Find the written hints, gather what the rite needs, and perform it in the right room to banish it.
- Something hunts you down there. Fight back, hide from it, or run for an exit.

### Loot and the surface

- Search crates and the dead, carry what you can, and climb out alive to keep it.
- Weapons, ammunition, bandages, smelling salts, batteries, chalk and valuables, each with a rarity.
- Your stash, loadout and the Store live on the surface. Whatever you take down and lose stays lost.
- Draw chalk marks on any wall to find your way or warn your party.

### Settings

- Accessibility: Reduce flashing (gentle lamp flicker, softer gunshots and hurt flash), press-to-switch Sprint and Aim, and Mono audio.
- Graphics: Render resolution and a Frame rate limit, for slower machines and laptops.
- Mouse: a separate Aiming sensitivity. Audio: mute the game while it's in the background.
- New help topic: Comfort and accessibility.

### Sound

- A new mix: gunshots are the loudest thing you'll hear (the shotgun more than the revolver), and the creature's steps are as clear as your own. Its death cry, falling stones and some small clicks were too loud or too quiet.
- Real recordings for opening crates, picking things up, chalk, torn protection and wounded creatures.

### Fixes

- You can no longer walk upright into the narrowing rock before a crawlspace or squeeze and see through the walls.
- Closed a gap in the rock at rubble dead ends.
- Much less network traffic during an expedition (about 40 times less), which helps on slow or busy connections.
- Smoother frame rate, especially in co-op: the catacomb draws in far fewer pieces, and other players' lamps only cast shadows when you can see their light.

### Known issues

- Balance (meter pacing, loot values, how dangerous it is) is still rough.
- Keybindings can't be changed yet.
