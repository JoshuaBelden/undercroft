# Code of Conduct

Undercroft's community (issues, pull requests, discussions and the beta) should be welcoming to everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

**Do:** be respectful and patient, assume good intent, give feedback on the work rather than the person, and accept feedback the same way.

**Don't:** harass, insult or demean anyone; make sexualised, violent or discriminatory remarks; publish someone's private information; or keep pushing after someone has asked you to stop.

**Reporting.** If someone makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile. Reports are kept confidential.

**Consequences.** The maintainer may remove comments, code or other contributions that break this code, and may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who does.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
